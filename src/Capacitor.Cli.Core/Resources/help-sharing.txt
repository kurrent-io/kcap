kcap sharing — see and change who can open something kcap shares, such as a page

Usage: kcap sharing <subcommand>

Subcommands:
  list <objectKey>                 Who it is shared with, and what you may change
  candidates <objectKey> [query]   Who you could share it with: each candidate's
                                   key and the relations it may take, the
                                   default one starred; says so when you may
                                   not share it
  set <objectKey> <audienceKey>    Give one audience a grant, or move the one it
      --relation <relation>        holds
  remove <objectKey> <audienceKey> Take one audience's grant away

KEYS

  The object key comes from the thing's own listing: `kcap page list`, `get`
  and `search` print a page's. Audience keys come from `candidates` or `list`.
  Both are the server's own; pass them as they are printed. A relation shows
  as its label with its id in parentheses, and --relation takes the id; a
  relation whose label is its id shows bare, and --relation takes it as shown,
  without the default's *.

  A server too old to send an object key cannot share from kcap: share from
  the web app there.

EXAMPLES

  kcap sharing candidates <objectKey> platform
  kcap sharing set <objectKey> <audienceKey> --relation <relation>
  kcap sharing list <objectKey>
  kcap sharing remove <objectKey> <audienceKey>
